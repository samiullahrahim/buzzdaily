Regina Hall Joins Jennifer Aniston and Peter Dinklage in Olivia Wilde’s Christmas Comedy “Naughty”

By BuzzDaily Staff
· 8 October 2026

Regina Hall is joining Jennifer Aniston and Peter Dinklage in “Naughty”, Universal’s Christmas comedy directed by Olivia Wilde. The casting, first reported by Deadline, rounds out an ensemble built around a family dispute with an unexpectedly magical witness — and it makes “Naughty” one of the most stacked comedy productions currently filming in London.

Aniston stars as Mallory, a mother fighting to secure custody of her son from her former partner. Her unlikely plan sends her in search of Santa Claus, played by Dinklage, in the hope that he will testify at her divorce hearing. Hall and Chase Sui Wonders play Mallory’s best friends, the pair who accompany her on the journey north, while Ike Barinholtz plays her ex and Samara Weaving — who joined the cast on 21 October 2026 — plays the ex’s mistress. Kate Berlant, John Early, Kate O’Flynn and Jamie Demetriou complete a deep bench of comedy talent around the central trio.

The film is written by Jimmy Warden, the screenwriter of “Cocaine Bear”, and directed by Wilde — her fourth feature as a director, after “Booksmart”, “Don’t Worry Darling” and “The Invite”. It is produced by Margot Robbie and Tom Ackerley’s LuckyChap Entertainment, the company behind “Barbie”, and sources who have described the project reach for the same shorthand: “Bridesmaids in the North Pole” — a custody battle and a road trip, played at full comic tilt, with the fate of Christmas-adjacent testimony hanging on whether Santa can be persuaded to appear in court.

The project has been a long time reaching the screen. Universal acquired distribution rights to “Naughty” in December 2023, and production is now under way in London in November 2026. That timeline gives the studio a full year of post-production runway: Universal Pictures has dated the film for a theatrical release on 5 November 2027, a date it will share with Warner Bros’ animated “Margie Claus” — a head-to-head start to the 2027 holiday season that sets up one of the more intriguing box-office contests of next year, live-action adult comedy against family animation for the same seasonal audience.

Hall arrives at the project in the middle of a conspicuous run. She recently appeared in Paul Thomas Anderson’s “One Battle After Another” and Peacock’s “The Five-Star Weekend”, and returned as Brenda Meeks in “Scary Movie 6” — a spread of prestige drama, streaming series work and broad studio comedy that mirrors the range “Naughty” itself is aiming for. Opposite Aniston, whose own run of ensemble comedy stretches back decades, and Dinklage, deadpanning as the most reluctant witness in film history, Hall’s role as one of the two friends along for the ride puts her at the centre of the film’s comic engine rather than its edges.

For Wilde, “Naughty” is also a statement of range: a big, cast-driven studio comedy after the psychological tension of “Don’t Worry Darling”, made with a producer in Robbie whose company has specialised in exactly this kind of talent-forward package. With filming under way and a prime November 2027 date locked, the next milestone is the first footage — and the first real test of whether “Bridesmaids in the North Pole” is a pitch or a promise.
