Contact Us

BuzzDaily Staff · Entertainment Desk · 30 October 2026

The quickest way to reach BuzzDaily is by email. Write to us at samiullahsgr787@gmail.com — whether you have a news tip, a correction, a question about our coverage, or feedback on the site. We read every message and reply as soon as possible; while we cannot guarantee an instant response, nothing sent to that address goes unread.

If you are flagging a factual error in one of our articles, please include the headline and, if you have it, the date it was published, along with what you believe should be corrected. Corrections matter to us, and the more detail you give, the faster we can check and fix.

If you are a publicist, representative or rights holder writing about a story we have covered, please use the same email address and make the subject line clear. We take requests relating to factual accuracy and image rights seriously and will respond promptly.

We do not publish any other contact details here. Please do not send personal documents or sensitive information by email; a clear description of your enquiry is all we need to get started.

Response times and courtesies. We aim to acknowledge messages within a few working days. To help us reply usefully, please put a short, descriptive subject line on your email — for example “Correction request: [headline]” or “News tip” — and keep any attachments to a minimum. We ask that readers do not send the same message repeatedly or to multiple addresses at once; one clear email is enough, and repeated copies slow us down.

What email is not for. Email is for editorial matters: tips, corrections, questions and feedback. It is not a way to commission changes to our coverage or to pay for placement — our reporting is independent, and we do not offer sponsored articles or guaranteed coverage. Advertising and business enquiries are also welcome by email, but they are handled separately from editorial work and never influence what we publish.
