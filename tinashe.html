Tinashe Scores the First Billboard 200 Top 10 of Her Career

By BuzzDaily Staff
· 7 October 2026

Tinashe's “Popstar” has entered the Billboard 200 at No. 6, giving the singer her first top 10 album and the highest debut of her career.

The set earned 35,500 equivalent album units in its opening week, according to figures cited by Music Times. Album sales made up 22,000 units, while the record's 16 songs generated about 13.7 million on-demand streams.

“Popstar” is Tinashe's eighth studio album, released on 25 September 2026 through Nice Life Recording Company, Atlantic Records and her own Tinashe Music imprint. The 16-track record — which arrived with singles including “Too Easy”, “Crash Out”, “Melatonin”, “Pillow Fight” and “I'd Rather Be Alone” — closes the trilogy she opened with “BB/Ang3l” in 2023 and continued with “Quantum Baby” in 2024, and it is her first major-label album since “Joyride” in 2018.

The milestone comes more than a decade after “Aquarius” reached No. 17 in 2014, and the years in between explain why it landed so hard. “Nightride” peaked at No. 89 in 2016; “Joyride”, delayed by RCA for roughly three years, reached No. 58 in 2018, and the label released her from her contract in 2019. Her independent run that followed — “Songs for You” (2019), “333” (2021), “BB/Ang3l” (2023) and “Quantum Baby”, which peaked at No. 199 in 2024 — kept her prolific but far from the top 10. “Popstar” did not just beat the No. 17 peak of “Aquarius”; it nearly tripled that album's first-week total of roughly 18,800 copies.

Tinashe's reaction matched the scale of the jump: Billboard reported her celebrating on X in disbelief at the biggest debut of her career — and the story has kept moving since. On 16 November, two songs from the album, “Melatonin” and “Too Easy”, were nominated at the 2027 Grammy Awards, a turnaround BuzzDaily traces in its Grammy nominations report.
