Kylie Jenner Stuns in $50 Million Fantasy Bra in Surprise Victoria's Secret Fashion Show Appearance

BuzzDaily Staff · Entertainment Desk
· 19 October 2026 · 8 min read
Illustration: Original artwork created for BuzzDaily

LOS ANGELES — Victoria's Secret spent the week teasing the return of its most famous jewel and refusing to say who would wear it. On Sunday night, the runway lights dropped to near darkness, a silhouette appeared wearing nothing the audience could make out except a pair of glowing white angel wings — and when the lights came up, Kylie Jenner was walking her first-ever Victoria's Secret runway in the Fantasy Bra, a diamond creation valued at $50 million and now, officially, the most expensive Fantasy Bra ever made.

The surprise was total because the secret had held. The brand confirmed earlier this week that the Fantasy Bra would return for the first time since 2018, but the model's identity stayed under wraps until the moment itself. Jenner — the reality star who became a makeup mogul with Kylie Cosmetics and one of the most-followed people on the planet — is not a professional runway model. That, of course, was precisely the point, and precisely why the room erupted.

THE MOST TALKED-ABOUT BRA IN FASHION HISTORY

The Fantasy Bra is the closest thing fashion has to a crown jewel ceremony. The first one debuted in 1996, worn by Claudia Schiffer, and across the following two decades the annual reveal became a pop-culture fixture — a single garment whose price tag, gemstone count and wearer were debated like sports statistics. The tradition then went quiet: the last Fantasy Bra walked in 2018, worn by Elsa Hosk, before the show and its rituals were shelved as the brand rethought its image.

That history is what gives this year's return its charge. Reviving the Fantasy Bra signals that the Victoria's Secret Fashion Show — itself back as a major spectacle — is no longer apologizing for its maximalist past. And the brand did not revive it quietly: at $50 million, the 2026 edition does not merely resume the tradition, it obliterates its price record, converting a nostalgic gesture into a headline no entertainment editor on either side of the Atlantic could ignore.

4,410 DIAMONDS AND MORE THAN 1,000 HOURS OF CRAFT

The bra was created in partnership with jeweler Jacob & Co., designed by its founder, Jacob Arabo. Fifteen craftspeople spent more than 1,000 hours building it. It is set with 4,410 diamonds in multiple shapes and sizes — including Jacob's proprietary Angel-Cut — arranged so the stones catch light with every step the wearer takes. At its center sits a 75.54-carat Fancy Vivid yellow diamond, the kind of stone that would headline an auction on its own. The finished piece weighs almost four pounds, which reframes Jenner's walk somewhat: this was less a lingerie moment than a red-carpet jewel given a runway of its own.

Arabo has been clear that the price was the floor of his ambition, not the ceiling. “I wanted to create the most expensive Fantasy Bra ever made, but value alone was never enough,” he said. “For me, it had to be about the diamonds, the light, and the movement... what matters most is the emotion it creates when you see it come to life on the runway.” It is a jeweler's answer to a fashion question — the piece was engineered to perform under lights, in motion, in front of cameras — and the silhouette-then-spotlight reveal was staged to prove exactly that.

The brand's leadership framed the collaboration as a marriage of two pop-culture institutions. Adam Selman, Victoria's Secret's executive vice president and chief creative officer, said: “When we decided to bring back the Fantasy Bra this year, I couldn't think of a more perfect person to design it than Jacob. I have been a huge fan of his work for a very long time, and he has played a big role in pop culture. I love his artistry, his craft, and his fearlessness.”

THE RUNWAY MOMENT — AND THE SISTER QUESTION

There was an obvious subplot walking down that runway with Jenner. In her family, the professional catwalker is her sister Kendall, a supermodel with Victoria's Secret history of her own. Kylie's empire was built on television, social media and lip kits, not castings. Critics of the choice could argue the Fantasy Bra belongs on a career Angel; supporters would answer that the Fantasy Bra was always a spectacle about attention, and no model working today commands more of it than Kylie Jenner.

What settled the argument, at least for one night, was the walk itself. Emerging from silhouette into full light, carrying nearly four pounds of diamonds in her first-ever appearance for the brand, Jenner strutted with a confidence that viewers at home immediately seized on. Fashion purists may keep their reservations, but as a piece of casting designed to relaunch an icon, it is hard to call it anything but a success: by Monday morning, the bra, the walk and the wearer were the fashion story of the day.

A PROUD FRONT ROW: KRIS, COREY AND TIMOTHÉE

The cameras found their second story in the front row. Jenner's mother, Kris Jenner, watched while filming her youngest daughter's moment on her iPhone. Beside the family sat Jenner's beau, Timothée Chalamet — the “Dune” star — whom Page Six spotted in the show's livestream smiling and clapping, in what the outlet described as a proud boyfriend reaction.

The sighting mattered because the couple so rarely provide one. Jenner and Chalamet have quietly dated since 2023 and almost never address the relationship publicly, letting appearances do the talking: his applause from the front row said more than any interview either of them has given. Between a mother filming on her phone and a boyfriend who could not hide his grin, the audience shots gave the night its human counterweight to all that carat weight.

“KYLIE PUT THE FANTASY BACK IN FANTASY BRA”

Fans, predictably, went wild. On X, one wrote: “I don't care what anyone says, she ate that up, her walk, the aura, the fantasy bra, she is that girl.” Another declared: “Oh she's the angel. The prettiest angel ever,” while others kept it simpler — “My jaw dropped!” and “She looks stunning, hello?” Under Vogue's Instagram clip of the walk, the comments ran the same way: “Absolutely gorgeous,” “I was so shocked over this,” and the line that may end up defining the night: “Kylie put the fantasy back in Fantasy Bra.”

Strip away the gemstones and the strategy underneath is simple. A revived show needs a moment that travels — beyond fashion press, beyond the livestream, into group chats and morning television. Victoria's Secret bought that moment with 4,410 diamonds, an eight-year absence and the most famous non-model it could cast. On Sunday night, glowing wings first and diamonds second, Kylie Jenner delivered it.
