Brandy and Monica Announce First-Ever Las Vegas Residency — The Boy Is Mine in 2027

BuzzDaily Staff · Entertainment Desk
· 11 November 2026 · 6 min read
Photo: Mike D. Photography / Wikimedia Commons (CC BY 4.0) · Brandy performing live on stage (file photo) · Free licence — full author and licence details on the Commons file page

LAS VEGAS — Brandy and Monica chose one of television's biggest stages to deliver the news. During a live performance of “The Boy Is Mine” on Dancing With the Stars Grammy Night on Tuesday night, 10 November 2026, the two singers announced that they will play their first-ever Las Vegas residency — The Boy Is Mine Residency — at Resorts World Las Vegas in 2027. The run comprises 10 shows across May, June and July 2027, produced by Black Promoters Collective, and general tickets go on sale on Friday 20 November 2026 at 10am PT.

AN ANNOUNCEMENT MADE MID-PERFORMANCE

The setting was the announcement. Rather than a press release or a social-media post, Brandy and Monica revealed the residency while performing the very song the residency is named after, live on Dancing With the Stars as part of the show's Grammy Night on Tuesday 10 November. It was a fitting stage for a pair whose defining record is a Grammy winner — and a characteristically theatrical way to launch a Las Vegas production. BuzzDaily has followed this season of the show closely, including its Super Bowl Night special, and Tuesday's performance now stands as the season's biggest music announcement: a residency, revealed between the verses of the song that built both careers.

THE DATES: 10 SHOWS ACROSS MAY, JUNE AND JULY 2027

The Boy Is Mine Residency runs to 10 shows at Resorts World Las Vegas: 29 and 30 May, then 2, 4 and 5 June, and finally 9, 10, 14, 16 and 17 July 2027. The run is produced by Black Promoters Collective. The shape of the schedule is worth noting: an opening pair at the end of May, a cluster in early June, and a return for five July dates gives the residency the rhythm of a summer fixture rather than a single weekend's event — three separate trips to Las Vegas for the production, and three separate chances for fans to build a trip around it. For a first-ever residency, 10 shows is a statement of confidence in the demand.

THE SONG THAT BUILT AN ERA

“The Boy Is Mine” was released in 1998 as a duet between the two singers, and it became one of the defining records of its decade. The song spent 13 weeks at No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100 and won a Grammy. Few collaborations in R&B history have carried a longer afterlife: the record linked Brandy's and Monica's names permanently, framed a friendly-rivalry story that followed both careers for years, and remained the reference point whenever either singer took a stage. That the residency takes its name from the song is precisely the point. This is a production built around the record that first put the two voices on the same track — and, nearly three decades later, it is still the title on the marquee.

FROM A SOLD-OUT TOUR TO A RESIDENCY

The residency follows Brandy and Monica's sold-out 2025 co-headlining The Boy Is Mine Tour, which demonstrated — city by city — that the demand to see the two singers perform together had survived every year since 1998. A residency is a different proposition from a tour: instead of the act travelling to the audience, the audience travels to the act, and Las Vegas residencies have become the prestige format for exactly this kind of catalogue show. BuzzDaily has reported on the city's pull for major live music throughout the year, from Celine Dion stealing the show at Metallica's Sphere concert to the stadium-scale residencies announced up and down the Strip. Brandy and Monica's arrival at Resorts World puts one of R&B's most storied partnerships into that headline economy for the first time.

TICKETS: GENERAL SALE ON 20 NOVEMBER

General tickets for The Boy Is Mine Residency go on sale on Friday 20 November 2026 at 10am PT. That is the date agreed across the sources for this report, and it is the only on-sale date BuzzDaily is publishing: reporting on any earlier presale windows conflicts between sources, so no presale date is stated here, and fans should work to the confirmed general sale. With 10 shows spread across three months of 2027 — and a sold-out tour as the most recent evidence of demand — the opening pair on 29 and 30 May and the July closing dates are likely to be the first to draw attention when the sale opens.

A FIRST, NEARLY THREE DECADES ON

There is a pleasing symmetry to the whole announcement. In 1998, “The Boy Is Mine” spent 13 weeks at the top of the Billboard Hot 100 and won a Grammy. In 2025, its singers took it back on the road together and sold out the tour. And in 2027, Brandy and Monica will do something neither has done before — a Las Vegas residency — under the song's own name, announced, fittingly, in the middle of performing it on live television. First-ever residencies are usually milestones of an early career. This one arrives as a celebration of a record that has outlasted every trend around it, performed by the two voices who made it.
