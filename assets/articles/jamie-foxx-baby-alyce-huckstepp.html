Jamie Foxx Welcomes First Baby With Australian Girlfriend Alyce Huckstepp — Days After Melbourne Cup Appearance

BuzzDaily Staff · Australia Desk
· 9 November 2026 · 7 min read
Photo: John Bauld · CC BY 2.0 · via Wikimedia Commons · Jamie Foxx at the Toronto International Film Festival, 2019 · Free licence — full author and licence details on the Commons file page (file photo)

MELBOURNE — Jamie Foxx is a father again. Sources have confirmed to PEOPLE that the Oscar-winning actor, 58, has welcomed his third baby — his first child with his Australian girlfriend Alyce Huckstepp, 31. The confirmation, reported by PEOPLE on 4 November 2026, ends months of public anticipation that Foxx himself had cheerfully fuelled, and it lands at the end of a week in which he was already the most talked-about international guest in Melbourne: dancing at the Melbourne Cup, talking about moving to Australia, and filming a big-budget action thriller in the city’s streets.

WHAT WE KNOW — AND WHAT WE DON’T

The confirmed facts are simple and, for now, deliberately limited. The baby is Foxx’s third child and Huckstepp’s first. Foxx is already father to two daughters from previous relationships: Corinne, 32, and Anelise, 18. Beyond that, the couple are keeping the details private — the baby’s name, sex and exact date of birth have not been revealed, and anything circulating beyond the confirmation itself should be treated as speculation. In an era when celebrity baby news usually arrives as a fully art-directed announcement, the restraint is notable: a confirmation to one magazine, the essential facts, and nothing more.

THE ROAD TO THE ANNOUNCEMENT

The news did not come out of nowhere. PEOPLE had confirmed Huckstepp’s pregnancy back in May 2026, after TMZ first reported it, and in September Foxx confirmed it himself on Jimmy Kimmel Live! with the kind of line only he could deliver: “I got a baby on the way… still got it!” The public timeline matters for understanding the couple’s week in Melbourne — this was not a secret being kept but a piece of happy news on a slow, managed reveal, which makes the timing of what happened at Flemington read less like coincidence and more like a celebration conducted in plain sight.

THE MELBOURNE CUP WEEK

Days before the confirmation broke, Foxx attended Melbourne Cup Day at Flemington on Tuesday 3 November — and behaved like a man with something to celebrate. He was seen dancing with My Kitchen Rules star Zana Pali in the Don Julio marquee, and he had flown Huckstepp’s family in as a surprise. Then came the quote that travelled around Australia: asked about his future, Foxx told the Daily Mail he plans to move here. “Of course, I’m moving. I’m a find a place,” he said. It is the sort of line visiting celebrities offer host cities out of politeness, but in Foxx’s case there is a personal foundation under it — Huckstepp is from Queensland — and, as it turned out, a newborn giving the sentiment a rather firmer basis than a race-day soundbite.

AN ON-OFF ROMANCE

Foxx and Huckstepp’s relationship has not run in a straight line. The pair were first linked in August 2023, then split in January 2025 after around eighteen months together, before reconciling later in 2025. The baby arrives, then, at the settled end of an unsettled story — a reconciliation that has become a family. Celebrity baby news lands differently depending on the couple it attaches to; BuzzDaily’s recent report on John Boyega’s marriage and the arrival of his baby boy told a similarly private story on a similarly public stage.

MELBOURNE, HIS NEW WORKPLACE

There is a professional reason Foxx is spending so much time in Victoria, and it is a significant one for the Australian screen industry. He is currently in Melbourne filming Deadlocked, an Amazon MGM action thriller directed by Nikhil Bhat, in which he stars alongside Sasha Calle, KiKi Layne and Luke Hemsworth. The production is using Melbourne as a double for New York, shooting across the CBD, Docklands and Port Melbourne — the kind of large-scale international production Australia has competed hard to attract, as BuzzDaily noted in its report on Bluefly filming on the Gold Coast. The numbers attached to Deadlocked are substantial: the production is expected to inject more than $36 million into Victoria and to create more than 855 local jobs, along with around 1,000 extra roles.

A NEW CHAPTER, ON TWO CONTINENTS

Put the week together and a picture forms: a 58-year-old star who, within a few days, has danced at the Cup, talked openly about relocating to his partner’s home country, been confirmed as a father for the third time, and gone back to work headlining a $36 million production in the city he says he wants to move to. Whether the move happens is a question only Foxx and Huckstepp can answer — “I’m a find a place” is a promise, not a lease — but the centre of gravity of his life has plainly shifted south. For now, the newest member of the family remains, by design, a private citizen: no name, no photograph, no announcement post. Just a confirmation, a very happy week in Melbourne, and a father who, by his own cheerful account, has still got it.
