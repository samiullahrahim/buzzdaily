Charli xcx Joins Martin Scorsese's Gothic Horror 'What Happens at Night' Alongside Leonardo DiCaprio and Jennifer Lawrence

BuzzDaily Staff · Entertainment Desk
· 23 October 2026 · 8 min read
Illustration: Original artwork created for BuzzDaily

LOS ANGELES — The “Brat” era isn't ending — it's evolving. Charli xcx has booked her biggest and most prestigious acting role to date, joining the cast of Martin Scorsese's gothic psychological horror film “What Happens at Night,” the Oscar-winning director's first feature since 2023's “Killers of the Flower Moon.”

The casting broke on Thursday, October 22, 2026, when Variety reported that the 34-year-old British pop superstar will share the screen with Leonardo DiCaprio and Jennifer Lawrence, the two Oscar winners who lead the film. Just Jared and TheWrap confirmed that Charli will appear in a cameo role, though exactly who she plays — and how much screen time she gets — is being kept tightly under wraps.

It's a small role on paper, but it's the latest and most deliberate step in what has become one of the most aggressive Hollywood pivots by any pop star in recent memory. Charli has spent the last two years quietly building a serious filmography while dominating pop culture with the “Brat” phenomenon, and landing a part in a Scorsese picture — any part — is the kind of credit that changes how the industry sees you.

INSIDE THE FILM: A SNOWY TOWN, A BABY, A BLURRING REALITY

So what is the film she's joining? “What Happens at Night” is based on the 2020 novel of the same name by American author Peter Cameron, adapted for the screen by Patrick Marber, the Oscar-nominated writer behind “Closer” and “Notes on a Scandal.” The story follows a married American couple who travel to a small, snowy European town to adopt a baby. But the adoption doesn't go as planned — and, in the synopsis's chilling phrasing, “as they struggle to claim their baby, they seem to know less about themselves.”

After checking into a largely deserted, eerie hotel, the couple encounters a string of unusual characters — a chanteuse, a businessman, a faith healer — and reality begins to blur in deeply disturbing directions, as World of Reel describes the unraveling. It's gothic psychological horror, a genre Scorsese has flirted with before but never committed to this fully, and the setup has drawn inevitable comparisons to everything from “The Shining” to the director's own “Shutter Island.”

AN AWARDS-SEASON DREAM TEAM

The ensemble Charli is joining reads like an awards-season dream team. DiCaprio plays “The Man” and Lawrence plays “The Woman,” and both stars are also producing the film. The supporting cast includes Mads Mikkelsen as “Brother Emmanuel,” plus Patricia Clarkson, Jared Harris, Welker White, Ilinca Manolache, and Gabriel Spahiu. It's produced by Scorsese through his Sikelia Productions alongside DiCaprio, Lawrence, and Daniel Lupi, with Apple Studios and StudioCanal backing it — the same Apple partnership that produced “Killers of the Flower Moon,” which earned 10 Academy Award nominations including Best Picture.

THE LONG ROAD TO PRODUCTION

The road to production has been long and, by Hollywood standards, genuinely fascinating. The project was first announced in April 2023, back when Scorsese was only set to produce Marber's script. In September 2025, everything changed: Scorsese committed to direct, with DiCaprio and Lawrence joining as the leads. The trio had originally been lined up for a Frank Sinatra biopic that was postponed the previous year, and Deadline's Justin Kroll reported that “What Happens at Night” was the project they had secretly been aiming for all along once Marber's script was finished.

DiCaprio later revealed that Scorsese had him rewatch Alfred Hitchcock's “Vertigo” (1958) as a “reference point” for the film — a tantalizing hint about the psychological terrain the movie will explore. And in a detail that only adds to the prestige, Jeff Sneider reported that the script received an uncredited rewrite by Paul Thomas Anderson at DiCaprio's request.

Principal photography began in the Czech Republic on February 24, 2026, shooting through Prague, the town of Úštěk, and locations near Kolín, with additional filming in Switzerland before wrapping in New York City. The film has been in post-production since late May 2026, with Scorsese's legendary editor Thelma Schoonmaker cutting it and cinematographer Rodrigo Prieto behind the camera.

CHARLI'S HOLLYWOOD TAKEOVER

For Charli, the Scorsese connection isn't entirely out of nowhere. She has been on a genuine tear as an actress: World of Reel counts seven films in two years, including a lead role in Pete Ohs' “Erupcja,” which she co-wrote, the A24 mockumentary “The Moment” (in which she plays a fictionalized version of herself and earned a story credit), Daniel Goldhaber's “Faces of Death” remake, Romain Gavras' “Sacrifice,” Gregg Araki's “I Want Your Sex,” Julia Jackman's “100 Nights of Hero,” and Cathy Yan's “The Gallerist.” A Takashi Miike project is also reportedly on the way. Earlier this year she spoke openly about her desire to act more — and in July, she put Scorsese himself on the cover of her album “Music, Fashion, Film,” alongside John Cale and Marc Jacobs, each representing one of the three art forms in the title.

WHY THIS CASTING MATTERS

Why does this casting matter? For one, Scorsese has a long history of spotting something in performers before the rest of the industry catches up, and his endorsement carries real weight with directors and casting agents. For another, Charli represents something specific: she's the British name in a cast of American and European heavyweights, giving the story a natural hook for UK audiences, while her enormous Gen Z and millennial fanbase in the US guarantees the film will be on the radar of viewers who might never have watched a Scorsese horror film otherwise. It's prestige cinema meeting pop culture at exactly the moment both need each other.

There's no release date or trailer yet. The working assumption among industry watchers — World of Reel predicts a Cannes 2027 world premiere — is that Scorsese will take his time in post-production, as he always does, and unveil the film on the festival circuit. A first-look image released earlier this year showed DiCaprio and Lawrence walking hand in hand through a stark, wintry landscape — DiCaprio sporting a mustache, darker hair, and glasses — and it established exactly the ominous atmosphere the film is going for.

One thing is certain: when “What Happens at Night” finally arrives, audiences will be watching for the pop star hiding in the ensemble. And if her cameo lands, Charli xcx's second act in Hollywood may be even bigger than her first act in music.
