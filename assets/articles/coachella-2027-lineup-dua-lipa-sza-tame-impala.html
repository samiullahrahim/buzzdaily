Coachella 2027 Lineup Revealed: Dua Lipa Makes History as First British Female Headliner as Madonna Returns After 20 Years

BuzzDaily Staff · Entertainment Desk
· 12 November 2026 · 7 min read
Photo: Justin Higuchi / Wikimedia Commons (CC BY 2.0) · Dua Lipa performing live on stage (file photo) · Free licence — full author and licence details on the Commons file page

INDIO, California — The poster landed late on Tuesday night, and by Wednesday morning it was the only document the music industry was talking about. Goldenvoice has announced the full lineup for Coachella 2027, and the desert festival's next edition carries a genuine piece of history at the top of the bill: Dua Lipa will headline the Friday nights, becoming the first British female solo artist ever to headline Coachella. Tame Impala headline the Saturdays and SZA closes the Sundays, across two weekends — 9–11 April and 16–18 April 2027 — at the Empire Polo Club in Indio, California. And tucked into the dance tent on those same Fridays is a name that stopped every scroll: Madonna, returning for her first full Coachella set since 2006.

THREE HEADLINERS, THREE ERAS OF POP

The headline structure follows Coachella's modern pattern — one headliner per day, repeated across both weekends — but the combination is unusually pointed. Dua Lipa takes Fridays, Tame Impala, Kevin Parker's psychedelic project, takes Saturdays, and SZA takes Sundays. Between them, the three acts map the last decade of popular music: dance-pop at its most precision-engineered, psych-rock turned festival spectacle, and R&B that has dominated streaming-era songwriting. More than 100,000 attendees are expected per day when the festival returns to the Empire Polo Club next April, and the top line alone explains why. Each headliner arrives with a distinct constituency, and Coachella has arranged them so that no single day owns the weekend.

DUA LIPA MAKES HISTORY ON FRIDAYS

The milestone belongs to Dua Lipa. When she walks out on the Friday nights of 9 and 16 April, she will become the first British female solo artist ever to headline Coachella — a barrier that has stood through the festival's entire modern era, despite British women anchoring undercard slots and guest appearances for years. It is the kind of first that reframes a career: headline status at Coachella is the live industry's clearest statement that an artist now sits in pop's top tier, and Lipa reaches it as a record-breaker rather than merely a booking. That her history-making sets share their Fridays with Madonna's return only sharpens the symmetry — the new record-holder and the artist who defined the template appearing on the same day's bill.

MADONNA RETURNS AFTER 20 YEARS

Madonna, 67, is billed in the dance tent on the Fridays — and the detail matters. This is her first full Coachella set since 2006, a gap of more than 20 years. She has not been entirely absent from the desert in between: she appeared as a guest during Sabrina Carpenter's headline set at the 2026 edition, a cameo that now reads as a deliberate trailer for this return. A full set, in the dance tent, reframes her as what she has arguably always been at Coachella scale — a dance artist first. Twenty years after her last full performance there, the booking is both nostalgia and argument: the festival is betting that the artist who helped invent modern pop spectacle can still command its most demanding room.

A BILL THAT SPANS GENERATIONS

Below the headliners, the lineup reads like a deliberate cross-section of the moment. Daddy Yankee brings reggaeton royalty; Phoebe Bridgers and Robyn represent two generations of indie-pop songwriting; Zara Larsson and Jorja Smith carry the bill's pop and soul threads. Weezer supply the veteran guitar slot, while JISOO of Blackpink underlines K-pop's now-permanent place on the poster. Don Toliver and Kehlani hold the hip-hop and R&B lines, Dom Dolla, Four Tet and Chase & Status anchor the electronic programme alongside Madonna's dance-tent set, and NCT and Måneskin round out a bill that reaches from Seoul to Rome. It is a lineup built to argue that Coachella's centre of gravity is breadth itself — and, as BuzzDaily reported when Ye packed 83,132 fans into Dallas stadium earlier this month, the live business is currently rewarding scale wherever it appears.

SZA'S TEASE, AND TAME IMPALA'S HOMECOMING

Neither of the other headliners arrives cold. SZA teased her headline slot days before the announcement, hinting at it on her private Instagram — a whisper campaign that had fans decoding the poster before it existed. She, too, warmed up in the desert: she guested during Justin Bieber's headline set at the 2026 edition, the same pathway Madonna took via Sabrina Carpenter, which suggests Coachella now uses guest spots as auditions for its future top lines. Tame Impala's return is the bill's homecoming story — the project last headlined Coachella in 2019, and eight years on, Kevin Parker returns to the Saturday slot as an elder statesman of the psychedelic sound that has since filtered through half the undercard.

DATES, SCALE AND THE 2027 LIVE CALENDAR

Coachella 2027 runs across two weekends — Friday 9 to Sunday 11 April, and Friday 16 to Sunday 18 April — at the Empire Polo Club in Indio, California, with more than 100,000 attendees expected per day. The announcement also lands in an increasingly crowded 2027 live calendar: BuzzDaily has already reported Brandy and Monica's first-ever Las Vegas residency and U2's 2027 stadium tour plans, and Coachella's April weekends now sit at the front of that year's season — the first major statement of what live music in 2027 will look like, and the standard the rest of the calendar will be measured against.

PASSES: PRESALE TODAY, GENERAL SALE FRIDAY

Passes for Coachella 2027 go on general sale on Friday 13 November at 12pm PT. Before that, an American Express presale runs today — Thursday 12 November — also at 12pm PT, giving cardholders a day's head start on the general rush. With a history-making headliner, a 20-year return and more than 100,000 people a day expected in the desert, demand when the sale opens is unlikely to be the story's weak point. The poster dropped late on 10 November; the coverage followed on 11 November; and by Friday lunchtime in California, the first chapter of Coachella 2027 will already be written — in the queue.
