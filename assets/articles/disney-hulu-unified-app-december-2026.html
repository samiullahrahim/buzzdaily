Disney Confirms Hulu Will Fold Into Disney+ in December — but the Standalone App Isn’t Dead Yet

BuzzDaily Staff · US Desk
· 23 November 2026 · 8 min read
Photo: Coolcaesar / CC BY-SA 4.0 via Wikimedia Commons · The Alameda Avenue entrance to the Walt Disney Studios, Burbank, California (file photo)

LOS ANGELES — Disney has confirmed that Disney+ and Hulu will become one fully unified streaming experience in December 2026. In a shareholder letter dated 17 November 2026, Disney CEO Josh D’Amaro and CFO Hugh Johnston told investors that Disney+ and Hulu subscribers will get a fully unified experience on Disney+ starting in December, including Hulu’s live TV service and select premium add-ons. The announcement is the final stage of a merger years in the making — but it is not, despite some of the headlines it generated, an announcement that the standalone Hulu app is closing. BuzzDaily explains what Disney actually said, and what it did not.

WHAT IS ACTUALLY CHANGING

From December, the two services’ worlds come together inside a single Disney+ experience. Hulu’s catalogue, its live TV service and select premium add-ons will sit within Disney+ for subscribers, rather than requiring viewers to move between separate apps. It is the completion of a strategy Disney has been building towards since Hulu content first appeared inside Disney+ in beta form. The December changes are not only about Hulu: Disney also says TikTok creator videos are coming to Disney+ in December, a sign that the unified app is intended to be a broader entertainment hub rather than simply two libraries shelved side by side.

WHAT IS NOT HAPPENING — YET

The accuracy point matters, because the story travelled fast under headlines suggesting Hulu itself was ending. Disney has not announced a shutdown date for the standalone Hulu app. Standalone Hulu subscriptions are still being sold, and the Hulu service continues inside Disney+. The confirmed change is the unified app experience: one Disney+ home for both services from December. Anything beyond that — a date for the standalone app to disappear, or a deadline for subscribers to move — has not been announced by Disney. Readers searching for whether Hulu is “being discontinued” in December should treat the confirmed news as a merger of experiences, not an immediate end to Hulu.

A MERGER YEARS IN THE MAKING

The road to December has been a long one. It began on 6 December 2023, when a beta version of Hulu content launched inside Disney+, the first visible step towards combining the services. Disney completed its full acquisition of Hulu in June 2025, following the Comcast deal that ended years of shared ownership and gave Disney sole control of the service’s future. In August 2025, Disney announced it would fully integrate Hulu into Disney+ in 2026. Then, in May 2026, Disney let eligible subscribers link their Hulu profiles with Disney+ — the practical groundwork for a single, unified experience. The shareholder letter of 17 November 2026 simply put the date on the final stage: December.

WHAT IT MEANS FOR SUBSCRIBERS

For subscribers, December is the moment the two apps they have been juggling become one place to watch. Those who already linked their Hulu and Disney+ profiles in May have done the groundwork; live TV viewers, in particular, will find their service inside Disney+ rather than in a separate destination. The consolidation also fits a pattern BuzzDaily has followed across the streaming business — from Netflix originals such as the Ben Affleck thriller Animals to the constant movement of library titles between services — in which the biggest platforms are racing to be the single app viewers open first. Disney’s bet is that one unified Disney+, carrying Hulu inside it, is stronger than two apps competing for the same evening.

WHY DECEMBER, AND WHY NOW

The timing completes a sequence Disney has followed in public for three years. The 2023 beta tested whether Hulu’s catalogue could live inside Disney+ at all. The June 2025 completion of the acquisition removed the ownership obstacle, giving Disney sole control of Hulu’s future. The August 2025 announcement set the direction, the May 2026 profile-linking did the plumbing, and the November shareholder letter has now set the date. December is also the heaviest viewing month of the year, when families gather around the biggest screen in the house — the natural moment to ask subscribers to form a new habit. From December, Disney is betting, that habit will have a single name: Disney+, with Hulu inside it.

None of that requires Hulu subscribers to do anything dramatic this side of December — and, on Disney’s own words, the standalone app has no announced end date. What changes in December is where the experience lives. Hulu’s shows, films and live channels get a new front door; the service itself, Disney is at pains to make clear, is not being switched off.
