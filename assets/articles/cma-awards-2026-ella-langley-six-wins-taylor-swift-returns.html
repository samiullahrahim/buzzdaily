Ella Langley Makes CMA Awards History With a Record Six Wins as Taylor Swift Returns to the Stage

BuzzDaily Staff · US Desk
· 19 November 2026 · 9 min read
Photo: BrDen, CC BY 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons · Ella Langley performing live in Grand Rapids, Michigan (2025)

NASHVILLE — Ella Langley did not just win the 60th CMA Awards on Wednesday night; she rewrote the record the ceremony keeps. The 27-year-old singer left Bridgestone Arena with six trophies from the 18 November 2026 ceremony — more than any artist has ever won at a single CMA Awards — on a night that also brought Taylor Swift back to the CMA stage for her first performance there in 13 years. The show was hosted by Lainey Wilson, returning as host, and aired live on ABC and Disney+, with streaming on Hulu from the next day.

Six wins, one night

Langley's six wins covered the whole top of the ballot: Entertainer of the Year; Female Vocalist of the Year; Album of the Year for “Dandelion”; Single of the Year for “Choosin' Texas”; Song of the Year for the same song, an award she shares with her co-writers Luke Dick, Miranda Lambert and Joybeth Taylor; and Music Video of the Year for “Choosin' Texas”, directed by Langley with Caylee Robillard and Wales Toney. She had arrived as the night's leading nominee, with nine nominations, and converted two-thirds of them into wins.

The scale of the sweep is what puts it in the record books. Langley is the first artist in CMA history to win six awards in a single ceremony, and the first woman ever to win all five of the night's top awards — Entertainer, Female Vocalist, Album, Single and Song — in one evening. The previous single-night record of five was shared by Johnny Cash, Alan Jackson, Vince Gill and, most recently, Wilson herself. The winners are voted on by more than 6,600 professional members of the Country Music Association, which makes a sweep of this size as much a verdict of the industry as of the moment.

Taylor Swift's return, 13 years on

Swift's performance was the night's worst-kept secret and its biggest moment. The 36-year-old had not performed at the CMA Awards since 2013, and had not appeared at the ceremony since 2016, when she presented an award to Garth Brooks. She returned with a medley that bookended her career: “I Knew It, I Knew You”, the song she wrote for Toy Story 5, and “Our Song”, the 2006 debut single that first made her a country star — with Keith Urban joining her on banjo and guitar.

The performance opened with a backstage walk-through studded with cameos from Brad Paisley, Little Big Town, Sugarland, Brooks & Dunn, Lainey Wilson, Eric Church and Bill Anderson, introduced by Kimberly Williams-Paisley. Swift had arrived on the red carpet in a strapless yellow Oscar de la Renta gown, with Brittany Mahomes as her date. She was nominated for Single of the Year — the 26th CMA nomination of her career — but the award went to Langley. Later in the night Swift returned to the stage in a different role, presenting Brooks & Dunn with the Willie Nelson Lifetime Achievement Award.

A tribute to Dolly Parton

The evening's most emotional moment belonged to an artist who was not there. Vince Gill and Lainey Wilson performed “I Will Always Love You” in honour of Dolly Parton, who died in August 2026 at the age of 80, as reported by InStyle and The Tennessean. Swift, who wrote a eulogy for Parton after her death in August and dedicated her MTV Video Music Award for Video of the Year to Parton in September, was seen tearing up during the tribute.

It was also a night built around returns. Wilson came back as host a year after her own five-win night had equalled the old record — and then watched that record fall, performed in the evening's tribute, and appeared in the cameos that opened Swift's set. Between the host, the returning Swift and the absent Parton, the 60th ceremony kept measuring the present against country's past, which made the identity of its biggest winner feel pointed: the future of the genre, collecting six trophies while its history was being honoured around her.

The rest of the winners' circle

Beyond the Langley sweep, Chris Stapleton took Male Vocalist of the Year, and Stephen Wilson Jr. won New Artist of the Year — the first CMA trophy of his career, on a night when his song “Gary” was also nominated for Song of the Year and Music Video of the Year. Stapleton joined Wilson Jr. on stage to perform a new duet. Musical Event of the Year went to “McArthur”, the collaboration by HARDY, Eric Church, Morgan Wallen and Tim McGraw.

The search numbers told the same story as the scoreboard. In the United States, “ella langley” was the top entertainment search of the day at more than 200,000 searches, with “taylor swift cmas” also above 200,000 and “stephen wilson jr” above 100,000 — and the CMA Awards also topped Canada's entertainment chart. For Langley, the night lands in the middle of an extraordinary week: only a day earlier she had been named among the leading nominees for the 2027 Grammy Awards. Country's rising class has a new centre of gravity, and on Wednesday night in Nashville, she collected the trophies to prove it.
