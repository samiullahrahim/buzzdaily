Foo Fighters Announce 2027 UK Stadium Dates: Two Wembley Nights, Darlington, Edinburgh and Dublin — Tickets on Sale 20 November

BuzzDaily Staff · UK Desk
· 16 November 2026 · 8 min read
Photo: warkoholic / Wikimedia Commons (CC BY-SA 2.0) · Dave Grohl performing with Foo Fighters at Lollapalooza Chile in 2012 (file photo) · Free licence — full author and licence details on the Commons file page

Foo Fighters are coming back to Britain in stadium-sized fashion. The band announced the UK leg of their Take Cover tour on Monday 16 November, confirming five huge outdoor dates for September 2027 — headlined by two nights at Wembley Stadium — in an announcement that immediately shot to the top of the UK's entertainment search charts, ranking number one on Google Trends with more than 10,000 searches and interest up 1,000 per cent. General sale opens this Friday, 20 November, and on the evidence of the band's last visits, the Wembley nights in particular will not stay available for long.

THE FIVE DATES

The run opens with the two biggest shows first. Foo Fighters play Wembley Stadium in London on Thursday 9 September and Saturday 11 September 2027, before heading north to Darlington on Tuesday 14 September and Edinburgh on Thursday 16 September. The leg then closes across the Irish Sea at Dublin's Croke Park on Saturday 18 September 2027. It is a routing that mixes the obvious — Wembley, Croke Park — with a genuine curveball: Darlington is not a regular stop on the stadium circuit, and its inclusion gives the North East a headline date of a scale the region rarely gets from a band of this size.

Wembley, meanwhile, is close to a second home for Dave Grohl's band. The Foos have headlined the national stadium across multiple tours and treat its vast bowl as a proving ground; two nights there, rather than one, signals the level of demand the promoters are expecting. Croke Park carries a similar weight in Ireland, as the country's largest stadium and a venue reserved for the very biggest touring acts. Bookending the leg with those two rooms, and threading Darlington and Edinburgh between them, makes this one of the most substantial UK runs the band has announced in years.

TICKETS: FRIDAY 20 NOVEMBER, 10AM

Tickets for all five dates go on general sale at 10am GMT on Friday 20 November 2026, via gigsandtours.com and ticketmaster.co.uk. As ever with Foo Fighters, the advice is to be logged in, payment details ready, before the hour — the band's UK stadium shows have a long record of selling through their first allocations quickly, and a two-night Wembley stand plus a rare Darlington date gives this sale three separate scrums at once: London, the North East and everyone willing to travel. Fans should buy only through the official sellers named above; the announcement coverage carries the usual warning that tickets bought through unofficial resale channels may not be honoured at the gate.

THE SUPPORT BILL

The support line-up announced with the dates is unusually strong. St. Vincent, Yard Act, Garbage and Florence Road are among the acts joining the tour, a bill that spans art-rock, post-punk and alt-rock royalty — Garbage alone are a stadium-grade draw in their own right, and Yard Act represent the current wave of British guitar music being handed the biggest stages in the country. Exact support allocations can vary by city, and fans set on a particular opener should check the listing for their date when tickets go on sale on Friday.

WHERE THE UK DATES FIT

The September shows are the tail end of a very long road. The wider Take Cover tour opens on 9 June 2027 in Reykjavik and runs through to the Dublin finale on 18 September, following the tour's 2026 North American leg. One point of clarity for fans comparing diaries: these September 2027 dates are a newly announced leg, separate from the European stadium run the band announced back in November 2025 for the summer of 2026, which included Liverpool's Anfield in June 2026. That run has already happened; this one is next year's return, bigger in London and broader across the country.

Whatever the city, the formula that has kept the band at this level is well established: a set that reaches across the whole catalogue, from the earliest singles to the most recent album, played by a band that treats a stadium like a club that happens to hold tens of thousands of people. It is why their UK dates have become fixtures of the live calendar — and why a September run, outdoors and under lights as the nights draw in, carries its own particular pull for the fans who have followed them from the start.

The announcement also lands in the middle of a remarkable 2027 UK live calendar. BuzzDaily has already reported on the Backstreet Boys' reported 2027 UK tour plans with a London O2 date and on U2's 'Greatest Show On Earth' 2027 tour and its presale — and the Foo Fighters' September block now gives the year a stadium climax to match its arena spring. For British live music, 2027 is shaping up as a year when the biggest rooms in the country are booked out by bands with thirty-year histories and something still to prove.

For Foo Fighters themselves, the Take Cover dates extend one of rock's most durable second acts. Three decades after Dave Grohl started the band as a one-man studio project, they remain what they have been for most of this century: the default answer when a stadium needs filling and a crowd needs a chorus. On 9 September 2027, under the Wembley arch, they get to prove it twice in three nights.
