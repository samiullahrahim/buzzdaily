The Game Awards 2026 Nominees: GTA 6 Leads With 8 Nominations Ahead of Its Launch

BuzzDaily Staff · Canada Desk
· 18 November 2026 · 7 min read
Artwork: Rockstar Games · Official Grand Theft Auto VI key art featuring Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval (editorial use)

The Game Awards has announced the nominees for its 2026 ceremony — and the list is led by a game that, at the moment the nominations landed, had not yet been released. Grand Theft Auto VI heads the field with eight nominations, including Game of the Year, after the full list was revealed in a livestream hosted by Geoff Keighley on Tuesday, 17 November 2026. Interest in the announcement was immediate: “game awards” was among the most-searched entertainment terms of the day in Canada, the United States and the United Kingdom alike.

Game of the Year: the six nominees

Six games — not the usual five — will contest Game of the Year: 007 First Light, Control Resonant, Grand Theft Auto VI, Phantom Blade Zero, Resident Evil Requiem and The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time, in its remake form. It is a field that mixes new properties, long-running series and a remake of one of the most celebrated games ever made, and the expanded shortlist gives the 2026 race an unusual shape: no single genre, studio tradition or era of gaming owns it.

GTA 6 leads the field

Grand Theft Auto VI's eight nominations make it the most-nominated game of the year, with its haul including Game of the Year, Best Game Direction and Best Narrative. The performance categories bring a further nomination with a footnote: Manni L. Perez is nominated for Best Performance for GTA 6 — although the nomination itself does not name her role, so the character she plays, Lucia, goes uncredited in the listing.

Nominated before it launches

The timing is the story within the story. GTA 6 launches on 19 November 2026 — two days after the nominations were announced. Its release date falls inside the awards' eligibility window, which makes the game eligible for the 2026 honours despite players getting their hands on it only after the shortlists were drawn up. By the time the ceremony arrives, the game will have been in players' hands for three weeks — long enough for the public verdict to form, but the nominations themselves were earned sight unseen by the audience that will vote with its controllers.

The chasing pack

Behind GTA 6, two games share second place: Control Resonant and Resident Evil Requiem follow with six nominations each, while Phantom Blade Zero has five. Between them, the four most-nominated games account for the bulk of the major-category attention — and set up the central contest of ceremony night: whether the pre-release favourite converts its eight nominations into the evening's biggest prizes, or whether the chasing pack, led by two six-nomination rivals, closes the gap when the envelopes open.

How the nominees are chosen

The shortlists are not drawn up by the show itself. The 2026 nominees were selected by a global jury of more than 160 media publications and creator outlets — a breadth of jury that gives the nominations their weight, and the same body of voters whose choices will decide most of the winners when the ceremony is held in December.

Ceremony night and the fan vote

The Game Awards 2026 takes place on Thursday, 10 December 2026 at the Peacock Theater in Los Angeles, beginning at 7:30pm ET / 4:30pm PT. Fans have a vote of their own: public voting is open now on the official site and runs until 9 December at 6pm PT / 9pm ET, and the nominees for the Players' Voice award — the ceremony's fan-decided honour — will be revealed on 30 November.

What the nominations set up, then, is a December with two overlapping contests. The first is the jury's: eight nominations give GTA 6 the strongest hand, but Control Resonant and Resident Evil Requiem, on six apiece, are close enough that ceremony night is no coronation. The second is the public's: the fan vote runs for more than three weeks, the Players' Voice shortlist lands on 30 November, and both will be read as a measure of whether the year's loudest release is also its best-loved.

For now, the headline belongs to a game the public has not yet played. When GTA 6 launches on 19 November, its eight nominations will be waiting for it — and on 10 December in Los Angeles, the jury's verdict on the year's most anticipated release becomes official.
