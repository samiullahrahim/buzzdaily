GTA 6 Is Out Today With a 99 Metacritic Score — and Its Secret Voice Cast Is Finally Revealed

BuzzDaily Staff · UK Desk
· 19 November 2026 · 9 min read
Artwork: Rockstar Games · Official Grand Theft Auto VI key art featuring Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval (editorial use)

The longest wait in gaming is over. Grand Theft Auto VI launched today, Thursday 19 November 2026, for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, rolling out region by region at midnight local time — with players in New Zealand among the first in the world to get in. No PC version has been announced. The game arrives 13 years after GTA 5, and it arrives carrying something no release has carried on launch day before: a 99 Metacritic score, and a voice cast that stayed secret until the credits rolled.

A 99 that ties the all-time record

The review embargo lifted on Wednesday 18 November at 11:00 UTC, and the numbers are historic. The PS5 version sits at 99 on Metacritic from around 147 critic reviews, every one of them positive; the Xbox Series X|S version is also at 99, from a smaller pool of reviews. OpenCritic shows a 99 Top Critic Average, with 99% of critics recommending the game. A 99 ties The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time (1998) as the highest Metascore ever recorded — and it puts GTA 6 ahead of its own predecessors: GTA 5 holds 97, and GTA 4 holds 98. One caution belongs with the headline: the score is a snapshot from launch eve, and it can move as more reviews are counted.

What the critics said

IGN gave the game 10/10. Reviewer Luke Reilly, who played more than 120 hours, called it “the single most impressive video game I have ever played” — a verdict reached on a standard PS5, running at a stable 30 frames per second. GamesRadar+ awarded five out of five; GameSpot, Forbes and Game Informer all gave 10/10; Eurogamer and VGC gave five stars. The criticisms that recur are narrow ones: a 30fps cap on every console, including the PS5 Pro — a limit Rockstar confirmed on 10 November — and some slow stretches in the story. Beyond that, the consensus is close to total.

The secret cast, finally revealed

Rockstar kept the game's voice cast secret through years of trailers and two delays, and the end credits have now done what the studio would not. Dylan Rourke has been named as the voice of Jason Duval: IGN Netherlands identified him in its review coverage, and Polygon's rundown of the end credits lists him in the role. Rockstar itself has not officially confirmed the casting, so it should be read as reported rather than announced. The search charts show how hungry fans were for the answer: “gta 6 voice actors” drew more than 50,000 searches in the United States, and “dylan rourke” climbed to third among UK entertainment searches.

Lucia Caminos, the game's co-lead, is played by Manni L. Perez — a casting confirmed through her Best Performance nomination at The Game Awards, announced on 17 November, and by the end credits. Stephen Root appears as Brian Heder. And then there is Mandy, a new character who has become an instant fan favourite: her voice actress has not been confirmed, and the widespread fan speculation that she is played by Australian streamer Kathleen Belsten remains exactly that — unconfirmed speculation, not fact.

Thirteen years, two delays

GTA 5 launched in September 2013. GTA 6 was first dated for fall 2025, then delayed to 26 May 2026, then delayed again to today. Players who pre-loaded the game needed up to 212GB of free space. The story follows Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos, a Bonnie-and-Clyde-style criminal couple, through the fictional state of Leonida and a return to Vice City.

The launch has dominated search on both sides of the Atlantic: “gta 6 rating” passed 200,000 searches in the United States, Canada pushed “gta vi game” up by 1,000% with more than 50,000 searches, and the term broke into New Zealand's top ten overall. In the UK, “gta 6 ps4” drew more than 20,000 searches — from players asking a question Rockstar has already answered, since no version for the older console exists.

The questions players are still asking

Two absences hang over the launch. There is no PC version — none has been announced — and there is no version for the older consoles: the game is a PS5 and Xbox Series X|S release only, however many players searched “gta 6 ps4” hoping otherwise. UK searches for “gta+”, Rockstar's subscription service, also jumped by 1,000% as players looked for launch-day extras. What players do get is the full game at midnight in their own time zone, after a pre-load that asked for up to 212GB of free space — the detail that told everyone, weeks ago, just how big Leonida was going to be.

Launch day is not the end of GTA 6's year, either. The game carries eight nominations into The Game Awards on 10 December, including Best Performance for Perez — an award that will be decided, unusually, after the public has had three weeks to judge the performances for itself. BuzzDaily covered the road here in two earlier stories, on the pre-load and on the nominations, linked below. Today, after thirteen years of waiting, the game itself finally takes over.
