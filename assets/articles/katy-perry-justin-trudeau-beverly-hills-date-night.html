Katy Perry and Justin Trudeau Step Out Hand in Hand in Beverly Hills — Her First Public Outing Since Russell Brand's Sentencing

BuzzDaily Staff · Entertainment Desk
· 13 November 2026 · 8 min read
Photo: proacguy1 / Wikimedia Commons (CC BY-SA 2.0) · Katy Perry performing live on the Witness tour, Montreal (file photo) · Free licence — full author and licence details on the Commons file page

BEVERLY HILLS — Katy Perry and Justin Trudeau were photographed holding hands as they left dinner in Beverly Hills on Wednesday night, in what is reported to be Perry's first public appearance since her ex-husband Russell Brand was sentenced earlier this week. The photographs, exclusively obtained by Page Six and also reported via DeuxMoi, show the couple leaving E. Baldi restaurant after a dinner date on Wednesday 11 November 2026.

WHAT THE PHOTOS SHOW

The images show Perry, 42, and Trudeau, 54, leaving the restaurant hand in hand. Perry wore an off-the-shoulder black top with light-wash jeans, black heeled boots and a small black purse, with pink lipstick and her hair worn in a bun. Trudeau wore a navy blue sweater, black jeans and black shoes, and was pictured opening a car door for her as they left. That is what the photographs show, and it is all they show: a couple who have been publicly together for over a year, leaving a Beverly Hills restaurant on a Wednesday night. Neither Perry nor Trudeau has commented publicly on the outing.

FIRST OUTING SINCE THE SENTENCING

The reason the sighting drew attention is the week it landed in. The outing is Perry's first reported public appearance since her ex-husband Russell Brand was sentenced on Monday 9 November 2026 to 10 years and six months in prison. Brand had been found guilty on 5 November at Southwark Crown Court of one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault, following a four-week trial. BuzzDaily reported the sentence in full in our Russell Brand sentencing report. Perry has made no public comment on the verdict or the sentence, and nothing in the reporting of Wednesday's outing suggests she addressed it; the significance of the timing is simply that this was her first time photographed out in public since the case concluded.

A RELATIONSHIP MORE THAN A YEAR OLD

By now, the relationship itself is well documented. Perry and Trudeau have been dating for over a year. Romance rumours first sparked after the two were seen at dinner in Montreal in July 2025. They made the relationship Instagram official last December, stepped out for their red-carpet debut as a couple at the Tribeca Festival in June 2026, and were together at the opening of the Lucas Museum of Narrative Art in Los Angeles in September 2026. Perry also reflected on the relationship in a September interview with People. Wednesday's Beverly Hills dinner sits inside that established pattern: not a reveal, but another ordinary public date for a couple who stopped being a secret more than a year ago.

TWO PUBLIC LIVES, TWO FAMILIES

Both arrived at the relationship after long, public partnerships. Perry previously split from her former fiancé Orlando Bloom, with whom she shares a 6-year-old daughter, Daisy Dove. Trudeau separated from his ex-wife Sophie Grégoire in August 2023, after 18 years of marriage. Those facts are the background the couple's public appearances now carry — and part of why a straightforward dinner date in Beverly Hills is photographed, published and read across three countries. A former Canadian prime minister and one of the biggest pop stars of her generation do not get anonymous evenings out, even on a Wednesday.

FROM MONTREAL TO BEVERLY HILLS

There is a geography to this relationship, and Wednesday's sighting completed a loop. It began, at least publicly, in Canada: the July 2025 Montreal dinner that first sparked the rumours took place in Trudeau's home city, the place where his public life was centred for a decade. Since then the couple's documented appearances have tilted south — the Tribeca Festival in New York in June, the Lucas Museum opening in Los Angeles in September, and now Beverly Hills. That arc mirrors the relationship's own trajectory: first photographed in the former prime minister's city, discussed in a September People interview by Perry, and now photographed as an established couple in the centre of her industry. The Beverly Hills photos are, in that sense, the least surprising images of the relationship so far — which is precisely why they read as a milestone of a different kind. A couple photographed hand in hand, opening car doors, leaving dinner: the coverage exists not because the relationship is new, but because of the week surrounding it.

WHY THE TIMING DREW THE CAMERAS

Celebrity photographs are rarely about the photographs. Taken on their own, Wednesday's images are unremarkable — and several of the outlets carrying them framed them the same way, as a date night. What moved the pictures from the gossip pages to wider coverage was the calendar: Monday's sentencing at Southwark Crown Court closed a case that had run for years in the British courts, with the guilty verdict on 5 November and the sentence of 10 years and six months handed down on 9 November. Perry's first reported appearance in public since that sentence, two days later and an ocean away, was always going to be noted. It is worth stating plainly what has not happened: Perry has said nothing publicly about the verdict or the sentence, before Wednesday or since, and no report of the Beverly Hills outing suggests otherwise. The record is a dinner, a hand held, a car door opened — reported here as exactly that.

NO COMMENT — AND WHAT COMES NEXT

As of publication, neither Perry nor Trudeau has commented publicly on the Beverly Hills outing, and there is nothing in the reporting to suggest either intends to. For Perry, the professional calendar continues regardless: she is scheduled to continue her Las Vegas residency "Play" at Resorts World Las Vegas. Wednesday night's photographs, on their own terms, show a couple leaving dinner holding hands. The story around them — a first public appearance in the same week her ex-husband was jailed for a decade — is a matter of timing, and BuzzDaily reports it as exactly that, and nothing more.
