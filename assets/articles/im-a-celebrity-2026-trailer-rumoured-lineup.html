I'm A Celebrity 2026 Trailer Drops — And The Rumoured Line-Up Has Divided Fans

BuzzDaily Staff · Entertainment Desk
· 18 October 2026 · 6 min read

The jungle is calling once more. With barely a month to go until the campfire is lit, ITV has unveiled the first trailer for the 2026 series of I'm A Celebrity... Get Me Out Of Here! — and while Ant McPartlin and Declan Donnelly look as mischievous as ever, it is the rumoured line-up doing the rounds online that has really got viewers talking.

The 26th series returns to ITV1 on Sunday, November 16, and if the trailer is anything to go by, producers are leaning into the festive spirit with trademark gusto. But behind the laughter, a familiar annual ritual is already in full swing: the guessing game over who will be swapping red carpets for hammocks this year.

What happens in the trailer

ITV dropped the first teaser on Friday, October 16, and it is pure Ant and Dec chaos. The Geordie duo stand before a choir in the middle of the jungle, conducting them through a camp reworking of "The 12 Days of Christmas" — only for the punchline to arrive in spectacularly revolting fashion.

"Oh, the Bushtucker trials are frightful, but the campfire's so delightful... I'm A Celebrity... Get Me Out of Here! Returning soon to ITV1," ITV captioned the clip, as the choir — and the presenters — were promptly drenched in what the voiceover cheerfully described as "12 tubs of offal".

"Good start!" Dec quipped, dripping. It is the kind of gloriously daft moment that has become the show's calling card, and it landed exactly as intended: fans lapped it up, and jungle fever officially began its annual build.

As ever, though, the trailer revealed precisely nothing about who will actually be heading Down Under. ITV guards its casting secrets fiercely, traditionally keeping the official line-up under wraps until launch night itself. That secrecy, of course, only pours fuel on the rumour mill.

The rumoured 12 revealed

Enter Super TV, the popular X account whose casting predictions have become something of an event in themselves. This week it shared a 12-name list it claims is the rumoured camp for 2026 — and it is, by any measure, a headline-grabbing selection.

Headlining the list is former England captain Wayne Rooney, whose name alone would guarantee monster ratings. He is joined by Big Brother host AJ Odudu, Kaiser Chiefs frontman Ricky Wilson, and ex-Little Mix star Jesy Nelson — four names with serious star power and, crucially, the kind of big personalities the jungle format thrives on.

Diversity dancer Perri Kiely and Gavin & Stacey actress Joanna Page bring the feelgood factor, while former Strictly professional Nadiya Bychkova and Love Island's Chloe Burrows add reality-TV pedigree. BBC presenter Ade Adepitan, Mis-Teeq singer Su-Elise Nash, Beta Squad YouTuber Sharky and former England cricketer Ben Stokes complete the dozen.

On paper, it is a classic I'm A Celeb blend: sporting royalty, pop stars, soap and reality favourites, a comedian-adjacent wildcard or two, and at least one name designed to make viewers reach for Google. Earlier in the year, former Prime Minister Sir Keir Starmer was said to be "odds-on" for a stint in camp, alongside Olympic champion Christopher Dean and Ibiza figure Wayne Lineker — but according to reports, that trio failed to make the cut.

Fans are split down the middle

If the reaction on social media is anything to go by, the rumoured cast has landed like a particularly divisive Bushtucker trial. Some viewers could barely contain their excitement.

"I actually know most of them, I can't believe it," one X user wrote in response to Super TV's list. Another added: "The fact I know 90% of them makes it suspicious to me." A third was more relaxed about the whole thing: "Oh I know 8 of them! To be fair though, I'm not one of 'those' people. I'll watch it whoever's on."

Others, however, were considerably less convinced — and rather more blunt. "I only know Wayne, AJ, Jesy, Joanna, Chloe," one admitted. Another cut straight to the point: "Who the hell is Sharky?"

Scepticism ran deeper than mere name recognition. "Wayne Rooney is highly unlikely sadly," one fan mused, while another poured cold water on the Nadiya rumour entirely: "I don't think Nadiya is going in as she is booked for panto at the beginning of December."

It is worth remembering, of course, that these lists are educated guesswork at best. Every year, at least a few "nailed-on" names turn out to be wide of the mark — and every year, ITV delights in springing a genuine surprise or two on launch night.

A tough act to follow

Whoever does end up in camp will have enormous boots to fill. Last year's series was won by social media star Angryginge — real name Morgan Sam Lee Burtwistle — who charmed the nation on his way to being crowned King of the Jungle, seeing off Celebs Go Dating's Tom Read Wilson and EastEnders' Shona McGarty in the final.

His victory felt like a watershed moment for the show: proof that in 2025, a YouTuber with a loyal online following could outlast seasoned television personalities in a straight public vote. It also set the template that this year's rumoured list seems to follow, with digital-native stars like Sharky and Chloe Burrows sitting alongside household names like Rooney and Joanna Page.

Across 25 series, I'm A Celebrity has perfected a deceptively simple formula. Take a dozen famous faces, strip away their stylists, their smartphones and their home comforts, then watch what happens when hunger, homesickness and the occasional kangaroo testicle do their work. The result, year after year, remains one of the most reliably entertaining spectacles on British television.

What to expect this time

If the rumours prove even half accurate, viewers are in for a fascinating dynamic. Rooney's presence would dominate the campfire conversation — few figures in British public life inspire such strong and divided opinions. Jesy Nelson, meanwhile, has never shied away from speaking her mind, and her post-Little Mix journey has been followed intensely by fans.

AJ Odudu would arrive as one of television's most assured presenters, the kind of contestant who tends to thrive in the jungle's strange social ecosystem. And Joanna Page, beloved as Stacey Shipman, would bring warmth and humour in spades.

The wildcard factor, though, may rest with the less familiar names. Every series has its breakout star — the contestant Britain takes to its heart precisely because they arrived as an unknown quantity. If Sharky or Su-Elise Nash turn out to be this year's revelation, nobody who watched Angryginge's journey last year should be surprised.

For now, though, everything remains deliciously uncertain. The trailer has done its job, the rumours are swirling, and the countdown to November 16 is officially on. One thing is guaranteed: when those campmates finally emerge from the helicopter and hear Ant and Dec's familiar greeting, the whole country will be watching.
