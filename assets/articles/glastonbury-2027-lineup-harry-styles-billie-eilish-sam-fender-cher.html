Glastonbury 2027 Line-Up Announced: Harry Styles, Billie Eilish and Sam Fender to Headline as Cher Takes the Legends Slot

BuzzDaily Staff · UK Desk
· 18 November 2026 · 8 min read
Photo: David Carroll / Wikimedia Commons (CC BY-SA 2.0) · Cher performing on stage during the Dressed to Kill tour (file photo) · Free licence — full author and licence details on the Commons file page

Glastonbury Festival has revealed the first line-up for its 2027 edition — and it has done so early, by surprise, and with a Legends booking that rewrites the record books. The festival shared its first 2027 line-up poster on Wednesday, 18 November 2026: Sam Fender, Harry Styles and Billie Eilish will headline the Pyramid Stage, and Cher will take the Sunday afternoon Legends slot, making her Glastonbury debut at the age of 81.

The Pyramid Stage headliners

The three headline nights are set. Sam Fender tops the bill on Friday, Harry Styles on Saturday, and Billie Eilish closes the Pyramid Stage on Sunday night. The poster — designed by Stanley Donwood — is the first official look at the festival's return, and it arrived without the usual build-up, shared by surprise on Wednesday.

For the headliners, the billing underlines how completely the Pyramid Stage now belongs to a new generation of live acts — three headliners whose audiences bought their tickets, in many cases, before a single name had been confirmed. Fender takes Friday, Styles Saturday and Eilish the closing Sunday night slot, the three biggest stages of the weekend divided between them.

Cher takes the Legends slot

The booking already dominating reaction is Sunday afternoon's. Cher's appearance in the Legends slot will be her Glastonbury debut, and at 81 she becomes the oldest performer ever to take the slot. It is also a genuine return to the stage: Cher has not toured or played a full show since 2020, which makes a Sunday afternoon at Worthy Farm — a set watched by one of the largest crowds in festival music — the setting for her first full performance in years.

The Legends slot has become one of Glastonbury's defining institutions, a Sunday afternoon honour previously given to Dolly Parton, Lionel Richie, Kylie Minogue, Diana Ross, Shania Twain, Burt Bacharach and, most recently, Sir Rod Stewart. Cher now joins that line as its oldest member — and, unusually for the slot, as a debutant, playing the festival for the first time in the very slot reserved for its most celebrated names.

66 acts in the first wave

The headliners top a first wave of 66 acts. Also unveiled on the poster are Massive Attack, Little Simz, Chase & Status, The xx, Lily Allen, Ezra Collective, Kraftwerk, Sombr, Basement Jaxx, Blood Orange and Bonobo — a list that spans electronic music, rap, indie and jazz, and that the festival says is only the beginning: more acts are to be announced, stage by stage, in the coming months.

‘An early musical taster’

Explaining the early reveal, festival organiser Emily Eavis said the festival wanted to give fans “an early musical taster” after the fallow year. Glastonbury took 2026 off — the festival's fallow year, the periodic break built into its rhythm — and returns from 23 to 27 June 2027 at Worthy Farm in Pilton, Somerset. The early poster is a deliberate break from the usual calendar: rather than letting speculation run through the winter, the festival has put its biggest names on the record seven months ahead.

Tickets already gone

Anyone hoping to be there will already need to be holding a ticket. General admission tickets for 2027 went on sale on 4 October and sold out in 45 minutes — before a single act had been announced. The line-up reveal therefore lands for an audience that bought blind, on trust, and has now been shown what that trust has bought: three of the biggest live acts in the world, and a Legends debut years in the making.

A first-wave poster is, by design, a partial picture — 66 names with the rest of the bill still to come. But the choices at the top of this one are the decisions the whole weekend will be organised around, and on that measure the festival has played its strongest hand early: the headliners are named, the Legends debut is booked, and the countdown to 23 June 2027 has a poster to pin itself to.

Further announcements will fill in the stages below the Pyramid in the coming months. But the shape of Glastonbury 2027 is already clear from its first poster: Fender on Friday, Styles on Saturday, Eilish closing Sunday night — and, on Sunday afternoon, the debut the festival has waited longest to announce.
