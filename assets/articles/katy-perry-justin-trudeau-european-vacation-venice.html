Katy Perry and Justin Trudeau End European Getaway Hand in Hand in Venice

BuzzDaily Staff · Entertainment Desk
· 24 November 2026 · 7 min read
Photo: mattbuck / CC BY-SA 3.0 via Wikimedia Commons · Katy Perry performing in Nottingham, 2011 (file photo)

VENICE — The most photographed couple in pop and politics ended their European getaway the way they have conducted most of it: in full view, and hand in hand. On Monday, 23 November 2026, Katy Perry, 42, and Justin Trudeau, 54, were photographed holding hands as they arrived at Marco Polo Airport in Venice, Italy, travelling by water taxi as they concluded a European vacation that had already taken them through Spain, Milan and Paris. Perry wore a camel-coloured two-piece outfit with a beige scarf, a brown hat and sunglasses; Trudeau, the former Prime Minister of Canada, wore an unbuttoned purple shirt over a black T-shirt reading ‘Will yap about books’, with grey pants — an outfit that managed to be both off-duty and entirely on brand.

A WEEK ACROSS EUROPE

The Venice arrival was the last stop on a trip that mixed his world with hers. On 17 November, Perry was photographed in the audience as Trudeau spoke at Atresmedia’s Metafuturo conference in Spain — the girlfriend in the crowd at a policy-and-media event, rather than the star on its stage. On 19 November the couple booked a hotel in Milan and stepped out in Paris, where they were seen at the Ritz, per Elle. Venice followed on 23 November, and the airport photographs closed the tour. It has been a deliberate-looking itinerary for a couple who, a year and a half ago, were still only a rumour: part holiday, part soft launch of a shared public life that now seems entirely settled. Trudeau turns 55 on 25 December, which gives the trip the air of an early celebration as well.

HOW THEY GOT HERE

The couple were first rumoured to be dating in July 2025, after being spotted together in Montreal, including at Mount Royal Park. What followed was a slow, unmistakable escalation. They made their relationship Instagram official last December, in December 2025. In June 2026 they made their red-carpet debut as a couple at the Tribeca Festival premiere of Katy Perry: The Lifetimes Tour. In September 2026 they appeared together at the opening of the Lucas Museum of Narrative Art in Los Angeles, and on 14 November 2026 they made their third red-carpet outing at the Baby2Baby Gala in West Hollywood. In a September 2026 interview with People, Perry spoke about the relationship, and about her ex-fiancé Orlando Bloom, with whom she shares her six-year-old daughter, Daisy Dove. Read in sequence — Montreal, Instagram, Tribeca, Los Angeles, West Hollywood, and now a four-country European trip — the Venice photographs are less a revelation than a confirmation: this is simply what the couple looks like now.

The European trip folded those two public lives into a single itinerary. In Spain, Perry sat in the audience while Trudeau addressed Atresmedia’s Metafuturo conference; in Milan the couple booked into a hotel; in Paris they stepped out at the Ritz; and in Venice they arrived by water taxi, holding hands, in front of the cameras that now follow them everywhere. None of it was hidden, and none of it was staged as an announcement — it was the pattern of the past year and a half, in which a relationship first whispered about in Montreal in July 2025 has simply been lived, in public, one red carpet and one city at a time.

THE SHADOW OF THE BRAND CASE

The trip also comes at the end of a grim few weeks in Perry’s wider story. Her ex-husband Russell Brand, to whom she was married from 2010 to 2012, was found guilty at Southwark Crown Court in London on 5 November on one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault, and on 9 November he was sentenced to 10 years and six months in prison. In newly unsealed court documents, Brand could not remember when he was married to Perry. Her first reported public outing after the sentencing was a Beverly Hills date night with Trudeau on 11 November at E. Baldi, where the couple were photographed holding hands — the outing BuzzDaily covered in its report on their Beverly Hills date night. Against that backdrop, the united front reported by Page Six in Italy, and the easy body language of the Venice pictures, read as a conscious choice: a new chapter, conducted publicly, on her terms.

TWO LIVES, ONE SPOTLIGHT

Trudeau brings his own history to the photographs. The former Prime Minister of Canada separated from his former wife, Sophie Grégoire, in 2023, after 18 years of marriage; they share three children. A former head of government dating one of the most recognisable pop stars on the planet was always going to draw cameras, and the couple have long since stopped behaving as though the cameras were a problem. Venice, in that sense, was a fitting finale: a city built on arrivals by water, a water taxi, two people holding hands — and a European vacation that doubled, as their whole relationship has, as a statement that there is nothing left to announce.
