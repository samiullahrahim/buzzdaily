Grammy Nominations 2027: RAYE Leads With 11 Nods as Rosalía, Ella Langley and Olivia Rodrigo Top the Field

BuzzDaily Staff · US Desk
· 17 November 2026 · 8 min read
Photo: RAYE performing at First Avenue, Minneapolis, 25 October 2023. erintheredmc, CC BY 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons.

The Recording Academy announced the nominations for the 69th Grammy Awards live on Grammy.com on Monday, 16 November — and the list belongs to RAYE. The British singer-songwriter leads the 2027 field with 11 nominations for her second album, This Music May Contain Hope., including her first-ever nominations for Album, Record and Song of the Year. The total ties the record Beyoncé set at the 2025 Grammys for the most nominations earned by a female artist in a single year, and it caps a nominations morning on which women dominated every one of the top categories.

Presenters including David Guetta, Olivia Dean, Pharrell Williams, YUNGBLUD, Carly Pearce and Kehlani joined the Academy's livestream to unveil the nominees across 100 categories and 12 fields. “It's a privilege to congratulate all the incredible music creators recognized in this year's Grammy nominations,” Recording Academy CEO Harvey Mason Jr. said in a statement. “From first-time nominees to artists reaching career milestones, it's inspiring to see musicians at every stage of their careers represented.”

The leaders: RAYE 11, Rosalía nine, and a four-way tie on seven

Behind RAYE comes Rosalía with nine nominations for Lux — every one of them, the Associated Press noted, her first nomination in that category, including Album, Record and Song of the Year. The Spanish star arrives days after winning Album of the Year at the Latin Grammys for the same record, a double BuzzDaily covered in its Latin Grammys report. Four artists are tied on seven nominations: Ella Langley, Miranda Lambert, Olivia Rodrigo and Phoebe Bridgers. Bruno Mars, Leon Thomas and last year's Best New Artist winner Olivia Dean follow with six each, while Taylor Swift, Geese and Kehlani have five, and Madonna, Cardi B, Brandi Carlile, Ariana Grande and Kacey Musgraves four apiece.

Album of the Year: an eight-way race, one man

The Album of the Year field is entirely female-led but for one record: RAYE's This Music May Contain Hope., Rosalía's Lux (Complete Works), Phoebe Bridgers' Lost Weekend, Olivia Dean's The Art of Loving, Ella Langley's Dandelion, Olivia Rodrigo's you seem pretty sad for a girl so in love, Taylor Swift's The Life of a Showgirl — and Bruno Mars' The Romantic, making Mars the only male artist nominated in the category. For Swift, the nomination extends one of the defining runs in Grammy history; for Langley, a first-time nominee overall, it completes a breakthrough year powered by “Choosin' Texas”, which recently set the record for the most weeks at No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100.

Record and Song of the Year

Record of the Year pits Dean's “Man I Need” against Langley's “Choosin' Texas”, Mars' “I Just Might”, RAYE's “Where Is My Husband!”, Rodrigo's “the cure”, Rosalía's “Berghain” (with Björk and Yves Tumor), Swift's “The Fate of Ophelia” and Tame Impala's “Dracula”, with JENNIE. The Song of the Year list — a songwriters' award — comprises “Berghain”, “Choosin' Texas”, “the cure”, Ariana Grande's “hate that i made you love me”, Swift's “I Knew It, I Knew You” from Toy Story 5, “Man I Need”, Mars' “Risk It All” and “Where Is My Husband!”. Best New Artist will be contested by Angine de Poitrine, Geese, Ella Langley, Slayyyter, SIENNA SPIRO, Don Toliver, Remi Wolf and The Womack Sisters.

Milestones, first-timers and five new categories

Beyoncé reached a landmark of her own: her Best R&B Solo Performance nomination for “Morning Dew (Donk)” is the 100th Grammy nomination of her career, extending her record as the most-nominated artist in Grammy history — even as RAYE matched her single-year mark. RAYE herself now has 15 career nominations and is still seeking a first competitive win. Among first-time nominees, the New York band Geese lead with five nominations, including Best New Artist and alternative and rock categories. Five categories make their Grammy debut this year: Best Asian Pop Music Performance — where SB19, BINI, LiSA, IVE, KiiiKiii and BABYMETAL with Polyphia are nominated — Best R&B Collaboration or Duo/Group Performance, Best Traditional Pop Vocal Performance, Best Traditional Folk Album and Best Latin Song. There is a BuzzDaily footnote in the dance field, too: Tinashe, whose Popstar gave her a first Billboard 200 top 10 this autumn, is nominated twice, for “Melatonin” (Best Dance/Electronic Recording) and “Too Easy” (Best Dance Pop Recording).

The shut-outs

USA Today's nominations report noted the notable names who were shut out entirely: Harry Styles, Justin Bieber, Ed Sheeran, Doja Cat, Hilary Duff and Lizzo all left the announcement without a nomination. As in recent years, the balance of the major categories tells its own story — of the artists with six or more nominations, only Mars and Leon Thomas are men.

The road to 7 February

Eligible recordings were released between 31 August 2025 and 28 August 2026. The Academy's nearly 16,000 voting members cast final-round ballots between 10 December 2026 and 7 January 2027. The 69th Grammy Awards take place on Sunday, 7 February 2027, at Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles, airing live at 7 p.m. ET / 4 p.m. PT on ABC — the ceremony's first broadcast on ABC since 1972, according to the Associated Press — as well as Disney+ and Hulu, with the pre-ceremony streaming on Disney+ from 2:30 p.m. ET. Between now and then, the race runs through the story these nominations have already written: whether RAYE can convert a record-tying 11 nominations into the wins that have so far eluded her, and whether Rosalía's Lux can do at the Grammys what it has just done at the Latin Grammys.
