MobLand Season 2 Finale Airs Today as Tom Hardy, Pierce Brosnan and Helen Mirren Prepare for Season 3

BuzzDaily Staff · Australia Desk
· 20 November 2026 · 8 min read
Photo: Gage Skidmore, CC BY-SA 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons · Tom Hardy at San Diego Comic-Con, 2018

SYDNEY — The Harrigans' second war ends today. The MobLand season 2 finale, episode 10, titled “The Queen Is Dead”, streams today, Friday, 20 November 2026, on Paramount+, where new episodes land at 3:01 a.m. ET. It closes a ten-episode season that has gripped audiences in Australia and New Zealand in particular — “mobland” was the number one entertainment search in Australia over the past 24 hours and number two in New Zealand — and it arrives with the show's future already settled. Season 3 is not a hope. It is a production, with cameras set to roll this month.

BuzzDaily will not spoil a finale that landed only hours ago. What can be reported is the shape of the season it concludes, the numbers it posted on the way in, and everything that is now confirmed about what comes next.

A SEASON THAT PEAKED AT THE RIGHT TIME

Season 2 has run weekly on Fridays from 18 September 2026, and it saved its strongest hand for the run-in. Episode 9, “Nervous Breakdown”, which streamed on 13 November, scored 8.7 on IMDb — matching the highest rating any MobLand episode has ever received. A penultimate episode equalling the series record is the kind of momentum finales dream of, and the search data agreed: in the United Kingdom, “mobland episode 10” and “mobland season 3” both sat inside the entertainment top ten over the past day.

For readers who came in late: MobLand was created by Ronan Bennett, with Guy Ritchie as executive producer and director, and it premiered on 30 March 2025. Tom Hardy stars as Harry Da Souza, the Harrigan family's fixer — the man sent to clean up the messes other people make. Pierce Brosnan plays Conrad Harrigan, the family patriarch, and Helen Mirren plays his wife Maeve, in many ways the most dangerous person in any room she enters. Season 2 introduced a new threat in Johnny Flynn's Frankie, while Paddy Considine's Kevin has remained the family's brooding centre of gravity.

SEASON 3 WAS CONFIRMED BEFORE SEASON 2 EVEN STARTED

Unusually, none of today's viewers need to sit through a renewal wait. Paramount+ confirmed season 3 on 3 September 2026 — before season 2 had premiered a single episode. The new season will run to ten episodes, filming begins in November 2026, and all three leads are confirmed to return: Hardy as Harry, Brosnan as Conrad and Mirren as Maeve. Brosnan has told Deadline that the cast have received the first three scripts, and Mirren, asked by Radio Times when work on the third season starts, answered simply: “We start next week.”

Behind the camera, the core team returns as well. Guy Ritchie, who executive produces and directs, is confirmed back for the third season, and a writers' room was already at work before the renewal was even announced — which is how the first three scripts came to be in the cast's hands while season 2 was still airing. For a production of this scale, that head start is the real story of the renewal: season 3 was being built while season 2 was still being broadcast.

What has not been announced is a premiere date. No date, window or year has been confirmed by Paramount+; the speculation that season 3 could arrive in late 2027 is exactly that — speculation, drawn from the show's previous production rhythm — and BuzzDaily will report a date when the streamer names one.

The turnaround also marks a change of pace for the series. Season 1 premiered on 30 March 2025, and season 2 did not arrive until 18 September 2026 — a gap of almost eighteen months between premieres. A third season filming from November 2026, only weeks after the second season launched, points to a shorter wait this time, even though Paramount+ has not committed to one. It is another reason the late-2027 speculation exists — and another reason to treat it as speculation until the streamer says otherwise.

THE FIRING STORY THAT WASN'T

The renewal also closed one of the year's stranger television stories. In the summer of 2026, reports claimed that Hardy had been fired from MobLand after alleged clashes with producers and with showrunner Jez Butterworth. Sources close to the production disputed the claim at the time, and by the end of June it was confirmed that Hardy would return for season 3. The reports should be read for what they were: an allegation, contested when it appeared and contradicted by the outcome. Hardy was not fired. As of this month, he is preparing to film the third season.

So today belongs to the finale. “The Queen Is Dead” is streaming now on Paramount+, the Harrigans' second season is complete, and — in a television landscape where most shows end a finale begging for their lives — MobLand ends this one with its next ten episodes already written into the schedule. Whatever state tonight's episode leaves the family in, Harry, Conrad and Maeve will be back. The only question the finale has to answer is what shape they will be in when they return.
