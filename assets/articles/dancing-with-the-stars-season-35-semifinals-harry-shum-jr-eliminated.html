Harry Shum Jr. Eliminated From Dancing With the Stars Season 35 Semifinals Despite Two Perfect Scores

BuzzDaily Staff · US Desk
· 18 November 2026 · 7 min read
Photo: Harry Shum Jr. performing on stage (file photo). vagueonthehow, CC BY 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons.

Harry Shum Jr. danced a perfect semifinal on Tuesday night — and went home anyway. The actor and his professional partner Jenna Johnson were eliminated from Dancing With the Stars in the Season 35 semifinals on 17 November 2026, one week before the finale, despite earning two perfect scores and tying for the highest judges' total of the night. It is the kind of result the show's format makes possible and its audience never quite gets used to: a couple at the top of the leaderboard, sent home on the night they could do no more.

A perfect 60 out of 60

Semifinal night asked every remaining couple for two dances, and Shum Jr. and Johnson answered with the maximum twice over. Their Paso Doble earned 30 out of 30 from the judges, and their Quickstep matched it — another perfect 30 — for a combined 60 out of 60. No couple scored higher with the judges all night: the total tied Amber Glenn and Pasha Pashkov for the best judges' mark of the semifinals.

The pairing of dances made the achievement starker. The Paso Doble, all drama and attack, and the Quickstep, fast, light and unforgiving of a single misplaced step, ask for almost opposite qualities from a couple on the same night. To take maximum marks in both is to deliver, on the judges' cards at least, the complete semifinal performance — and perfect scores at this stage of the competition are rare enough that two in one evening would ordinarily be read as a ticket to the finale.

Shum Jr. and Johnson had been building towards it for weeks. The pair arrived at the semifinals having topped the leaderboard for three consecutive weeks — the form couple of the season's closing stretch, and, on the evidence of Tuesday's scoresheet, still improving when it mattered most.

Where the night was actually decided

Dancing With the Stars does not send its couples home on judges' scores alone. The judges' marks are combined with viewer votes, and it was in that combination that Shum Jr. and Johnson fell short: despite the perfect 60, the pair did not receive enough combined support to survive the semifinal. When the final two couples were left standing, Shum Jr. and Johnson found themselves alongside Ezra Frech — and it was Frech who was saved, and the perfect scorers who went home.

The result underlines the oldest truth of the format. The judges can only take a couple so far; by the semifinals, the audience's vote carries decisive weight alongside a flawless dance, and a couple that tops the leaderboard can still be one quiet voting night away from elimination. Shum Jr. and Johnson's exit is the sharpest illustration of that rule the season has produced: nothing in their dancing failed on Tuesday. The arithmetic did.

The road to the semifinal

The field had been narrowing week by week. The previous elimination, on 10 November's Grammy Night, sent Jenna Dewan home, leaving six couples to contest the semifinals. Shum Jr. and Johnson's exit, one week before the finale, removes the couple that had led the judges' leaderboard for three straight weeks — and leaves a final five without the season's most consistent scorers.

The five couples in the finale

Five couples will contest the Season 35 finale: Amber Glenn and Pasha Pashkov, who matched the night's top judges' total; Maura Higgins and Mark Ballas; Ezra Frech and Daniella Karagach, saved from the final two at Shum Jr. and Johnson's expense; Tatyana Ali and Jan Ravnik; and Connor Wood and Rylee Arnold.

Wood and Arnold's place in the line-up tells the other half of the semifinal story. They advanced despite posting the lowest judges' total of the night, 48 out of 60 — twelve points behind the couple who went home. On a night when the judges' marks ranged from perfection to 48, it was the public vote, not the leaderboard, that drew the line between the finalists and the eliminated.

Finale night: Tuesday 24 November

The Season 35 finale takes place on Tuesday, 24 November 2026, one week after the semifinals. It will air as a three-hour live telecast, from 8pm to 11pm ET, on ABC and Disney+, at the end of which one couple will lift the Len Goodman Mirrorball Trophy.

Shum Jr. and Johnson will watch it from the audience side of the ballroom. Their semifinal remains the season's defining scoresheet — two perfect dances, the joint-highest judges' total of the night, three weeks at the top of the leaderboard — and a reminder, delivered in the cruellest week possible, that on Dancing With the Stars a perfect score is a performance, not a guarantee.
