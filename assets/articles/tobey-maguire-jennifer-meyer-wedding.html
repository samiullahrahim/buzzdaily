Tobey Maguire Officiates Jennifer Meyer's Hawaii Wedding to Geoffrey Ogunlesi

BuzzDaily Staff · Entertainment Desk
· 12 October 2026 · 7 min read
Featured artwork could not be produced for this edition. The report below is fully sourced.

Tobey Maguire stood at the center of an unusually modern Hollywood family moment in Hawaii over the weekend. The actor officiated the wedding of his former wife, jewelry designer Jennifer Meyer, to Geoffrey Ogunlesi at the Four Seasons Resort Lanai on Saturday, October 10 — a ceremonial role that made the celebration a story far beyond its star-filled guest list.

The facts are striking without embellishment. Meyer, 49, exchanged vows with Ogunlesi, the son of Nigerian billionaire Adebayo Ogunlesi, while Maguire, 51, led the ceremony. The wedding took place less than three weeks after Maguire and Meyer were legally declared single on September 21, following a divorce process that had extended for years.

That timing explains why the photographs drew such immediate attention. Celebrity weddings routinely produce coverage of clothes, locations and famous guests. Here, however, the person presiding over the vows carried the story: Meyer's former husband was not merely present as a co-parent or invited friend. He had accepted a formal and highly visible place in the ceremony.

A FAMILY EVENT WITH HOLLYWOOD GUESTS

The Lanai gathering brought together the couple's family and a recognisable group of friends from entertainment and business. Parade reported that Courteney Cox and Rachel Zoe attended, along with Leonardo DiCaprio and his girlfriend, Vittoria Ceretti. Jeff Bezos and Lauren Sánchez Bezos were also among the guests.

All three of Meyer's children were there. Ruby, 19, and Otis, 17 — the two children she shares with Maguire — attended alongside Meyer's infant daughter with Ogunlesi. Their presence is important to the meaning of the day. It placed the ceremony within an extended family that spans an earlier marriage and a new one, rather than presenting those chapters as separate worlds.

The location supplied a destination-wedding setting, but reporting available after the ceremony focused far more on the people than on decorative details. That restraint matters: the verified story is not a catalogue of private moments. It is the public fact that a former couple, their children and a new spouse shared a significant family occasion with Maguire acting as officiant.

THE LONG ROAD FROM MARRIAGE TO DIVORCE

Maguire and Meyer met in 2003 while he was filming Seabiscuit. They married in 2007 and later became parents to Ruby and Otis. In October 2016, the pair announced their separation, ending their life as a couple while continuing to raise their children.

Meyer filed for divorce in 2020, four years after the separation announcement. The legal process continued until September 21, 2026, when their marital status was formally ended. According to the court-document reporting cited by Parade, the agreement to terminate that status did not necessarily resolve every remaining legal and financial issue at the same time.

That distinction makes the wedding's proximity to the legal ruling feel especially notable, but it should not be mistaken for evidence of sudden goodwill. A cooperative public relationship between Maguire and Meyer has had years to develop since their 2016 separation. The officiant role was surprising to outsiders because it became visible all at once; the trust required for it would ordinarily be built over a much longer period.

WHY THE OFFICIANT ROLE RESONATED

Reaction gathered quickly after photographs of the ceremony appeared on PEOPLE's Instagram account. Some commenters were puzzled by the choice and reduced their response to a simple question: “but why?” For them, the emotional symbolism of marriage vows seemed difficult to separate from the history shared by Meyer and Maguire.

Others viewed the same scene as evidence that divorce does not have to erase affection, respect or family cooperation. One supportive reaction described it as “a level of healthy most of us aren't used to,” while another praised the former couple rather than treating the arrangement as inherently awkward.

Those responses reveal how much the public still relies on a conflict-based script for celebrity breakups. Entertainment coverage often frames former partners through feuds, custody disputes or carefully avoided encounters. A ceremony in which an ex-spouse takes on a trusted role interrupts that familiar storyline, so surprise becomes part of the headline even when no conflict is present.

There is also a practical family dimension. Meyer and Maguire share two teenagers, and the wedding joined them with Meyer's youngest child and her new husband. An officiant does more than stand in photographs; the person carries responsibility for guiding a major life ritual. Maguire's participation therefore communicated public support for a family transition that directly involves his children.

That does not make the arrangement a universal model, and the online debate need not produce one. Former couples have different histories, boundaries and levels of contact. What appears generous in one family could be uncomfortable or inappropriate in another. The significance of this ceremony lies precisely in its specificity: the people involved evidently chose a form that worked for them.

WHAT THE MOMENT SAYS — AND WHAT IT DOES NOT

It would be easy to turn the wedding into a sweeping lesson about “conscious uncoupling” or ideal post-divorce behavior. The available facts support a narrower conclusion. Maguire was asked to officiate, he did so, and the family assembled together in Hawaii. Beyond that, the private conversations and decisions that led to the arrangement remain private.

Still, public gestures have public meaning. The ceremony showed that the legal end of a marriage does not automatically require the end of every bond created by it. Meyer and Maguire's relationship changed, but their shared responsibility as parents continued. At the wedding, that continuity was visible without displacing Ogunlesi or diminishing the new marriage being celebrated.

The famous names in attendance guaranteed attention, yet none of them became the defining image. What held readers was the sight of a former husband helping mark the beginning of his ex-wife's new marriage. The scene felt surprising because it resisted the usual categories: it was neither reconciliation nor rivalry, neither distance nor denial of the past.

For Meyer and Ogunlesi, the ceremony was first and foremost their wedding. For observers, it became a rare example of a blended family's history being acknowledged rather than edited out. Maguire's role did not rewrite the former marriage; it showed that its end could coexist with respect for what followed.

That may be why admiration ultimately sat beside disbelief in the reaction. The arrangement was unconventional, but it appeared intentional. In a culture accustomed to treating divorce as a permanent dividing line, the most memorable part of the Lanai celebration was the decision to let an old family connection support a new one.
