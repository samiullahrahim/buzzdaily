Celebrity Traitors Turns a Round Table Cliffhanger Into Event Television

By BuzzDaily Staff
· 8 October 2026

The Celebrity Traitors has turned an abrupt Round Table ending into the week’s biggest British reality-TV conversation. The previous episode stopped before a single banishment vote was cast, leaving viewers to debate whether the pause was strategy or pure scheduling theatre.

The answer arrives in the next BBC One episode, which continues the social deduction game and its central question: who can persuade the room before suspicion hardens into a vote?

The format’s strength remains its pressure-cooker simplicity — famous faces, incomplete information and a room in which every hesitation can become evidence.
