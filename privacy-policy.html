Privacy Policy

BuzzDaily Staff · Entertainment Desk · 30 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BuzzDaily handles information when you visit our site. We keep data collection to a minimum, and we never sell your personal information.

ANALYTICS

We use Google Analytics (GA4) to measure traffic on the site — for example, which stories readers visit and how they find them. Analytics data is aggregated and helps us understand what our readers enjoy; it is not used to identify you personally.

ADVERTISING

We show ads through Google AdSense. AdSense and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to personalise the ads you see, for example based on sites you have visited. These advertising cookies are set by Google and are subject to Google's own privacy policies. You can manage ad personalisation at any time through your Google ad settings.

COOKIES

In short, the site uses a small number of cookies: analytics cookies from Google Analytics and advertising cookies from Google AdSense. Your browser lets you block or delete cookies at any time; doing so may change how some parts of the site behave.

WHAT WE DO NOT DO

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect names, addresses or payment details.

YOUR CHOICES

You can opt out of Google Analytics measurement by installing Google's official opt-out browser add-on, and you can turn off ad personalisation at any time in your Google ad settings. Deleting cookies in your browser will remove the cookies this site has set, though new ones may be set on your next visit.

CONTACT
